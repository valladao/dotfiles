# Decomposition Quality Checklist

## Traceability
- [ ] Every epic maps to PRD goals.
- [ ] Every story maps to acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Out-of-scope items are excluded or explicitly marked.

## Actionability
- [ ] Tasks are concrete and implementation-ready.
- [ ] No vague tasks ("improve", "optimize") without metric.
- [ ] Dependencies and blockers are explicit.

## Prioritization
- [ ] P0/P1/P2 is consistent with PRD criticality.
- [ ] Sequence enables incremental delivery.
- [ ] Milestones are realistic for team capacity.

## Engineering Quality
- [ ] Includes testing/validation tasks.
- [ ] Includes observability and analytics tasks.
- [ ] Includes security/privacy tasks when relevant.

## Delivery Safety
- [ ] Rollout and rollback steps are represented.
- [ ] High-risk items have mitigations and owners.
- [ ] Open questions are isolated from committed scope.
